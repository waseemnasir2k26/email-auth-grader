Cold-email send guidance

GUIDANCE AND RULES OF THUMB - NOT A DNS CHECK

  • Send cold outreach from a separate subdomain or secondary domain (for example mail.dmarc-none.example), not your primary domain, so a reputation problem cannot hit your everyday mail.
  • Warm up new mailboxes: start with a handful of messages a day and raise volume gradually over 3-4 weeks. Keep daily volume per mailbox modest; there is no official number, so treat any figure as a rule of thumb.
  • Watch Google Postmaster Tools. Google's published guidance is to keep the user-reported spam rate under 0.30% (and ideally under 0.10%).
  • Add a working unsubscribe/opt-out to every campaign. Bulk marketing mail to Gmail and Yahoo needs one-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058): check a real message with `email-auth-grader headers message.eml`.
  • Authenticate with the same domain you put in the From header (alignment) and verify DNS changes before launching a campaign.
  • Keep DMARC at p=none only while you collect reports; plan the move to quarantine.
  • Fix the high and medium findings above before sending any volume from this domain.
